Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) is an approach that combines a retriever and a generator to answer questions using external knowledge sources. 
The retriever searches a vector database for the most relevant document chunks based on a user query. 
The generator, typically a large language model, then uses those retrieved chunks as context to produce an accurate and grounded answer. 
RAG improves factual correctness and reduces hallucination compared to a standalone language model.
