LLaMA, developed by Meta AI, is a family of efficient language models (e.g., LLaMA-7B, 13B, 70B) optimized for research. Known for high performance with fewer parameters, 
it excels in tasks like text generation and question answering. Accessible via Hugging Face’s `transformers` library, 
LLaMA is ideal for retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) when paired with vector databases like Qdrant.
