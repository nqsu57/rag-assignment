**Embedding Models for Qdrant in RAG Pipelines**  
Embedding models, such as those from Hugging Face’s `sentence-transformers` library, are critical for generating dense vector representations of text in Python-based retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) pipelines with Qdrant. 
Models like `all-MiniLM-L6-v2` or `multi-qa-MiniLM-L6-cos-v1` transform text chunks into fixed-size vectors (e.g., 384 dimensions), capturing semantic meaning for storage in Qdrant’s vector database. 
In Python, the process involves loading a model with `SentenceTransformer`, encoding document chunks or queries into embeddings, and upserting them into a Qdrant collection using `qdrant-client`.
During retrieval, the same model embeds the query, enabling Qdrant’s HNSW indexing to find similar vectors for LLM context.
These lightweight, efficient models balance performance and accuracy, making them ideal for scalable RAG applications, with Python’s `numpy` and `torch` libraries streamlining vector operations and model inference.
